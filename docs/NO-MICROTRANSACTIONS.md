# No Micro-transactions. Ever.

This is a permanent, public commitment.

Ship-receipts will never introduce micro-transactions, paid boosts, premium XP multipliers, purchasable streaks, or any mechanic that allows money to buy a scoring advantage.

---

## Why this matters

Micro-transactions in productivity and habit tools are a category error. They convert an accountability system into a pay-to-win game. They undermine the core premise — that your receipts reflect real work, not financial investment.

Ship-receipts is a ledger of what you shipped. A dollar cannot make something shipped that wasn't.

---

## What this means in practice

- No premium tier with higher multipliers
- No paid "streak freeze" tokens
- No purchasable badges or cosmetic rankings
- No sponsored receipt boosts
- No "pro" plan that changes scoring

Monetization, if it ever exists, will be in the form of infrastructure (hosting, storage, federation) — never in the scoring layer.

---

## This commitment is permanent

This document is version-controlled. Any attempt to remove or weaken this commitment will be visible in the git history and subject to community scrutiny.
